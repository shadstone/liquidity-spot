The two sides

Your asset decides your role

It does not matter who posted the offer. The person selling HNS is always called Alice, and the person buying HNS is always called Bob. Every trade room also spells this out in plain language.

Alice · HNS seller

SENDS HNS

Alice gives HNS and receives BTC. Alice may be either the offer creator or the person accepting it.

Bob · HNS buyer

SENDS BTC

Bob gives BTC and receives HNS. “Bob” is a trade role here—it does not mean the Bob Wallet app.

Main product path

A P2P trade from start to finish

Follow the room in order. Its large green button changes to show the current shared next step.

  1. 1

    Choose an offer or post your own

    Browse the open P2P offers. If none fit, choose Buy HNS or Sell HNS, enter the amount and rate, and post an offer. Posting creates a public listing—it does not move funds.

  2. 2

    Check the offer before accepting

    Review the HNS amount, BTC/HNS rate, quoted BTC total, payment method, offer note, and any maker Gems bond. Accept only if you can take the opposite side.

  3. 3

    Enter the shared trade room

    Accepting matches the offer and opens one room for both parties. At the top, check your role plus exactly what you send and receive. Acceptance still does not transfer HNS or BTC.

  4. 4

    Most important step

    Confirm the plan before sending

    Use room chat to confirm both receiving addresses, the exact whole-satoshi BTC total, who sends first, and how many confirmations you will wait for. Human P2P does not force Alice or Bob to send first—the two parties must agree.

  5. 5

    Record each verified milestone

    The room guides both parties through First transfer, Verify, and Other side. Add public TXIDs or confirmations to chat. Click the next-step button only after the described event really happened.

  6. 6

    Verify, complete, and leave feedback

    After both people independently verify that the expected HNS and BTC arrived, mark the trade complete. Then leave factual feedback and download a PDF or text receipt if you need a record.

Common questions

If something does not look right

My offer is posted, but I do not have a trade room

The offer is still waiting for another person. Open its shareable page and send the permalink to someone who can take the opposite side. A room appears only after acceptance.

I am not sure whether I am buying or selling

Read the “Your role,” “You send,” and “You receive” cards at the top of the room. Do not proceed until those three cards match your intention.

The other person stopped replying or changed the deal

Do not send more funds. Write the facts in room chat, then use “Open a dispute” near the bottom of the room. Manual correction and no-show controls are also kept there.

I lost access to my guest trades

Use the Guest Recovery option on the login page with the recovery key you saved. Liquidity.spot cannot reconstruct a lost recovery key.

Experimental · testing only

Atomic swaps are a separate advanced mode

The Atomic Swaps pages describe an HTLC flow with hashes, timelocks, wallet scripts, and a fixed on-chain order. This is not the normal P2P room described above.

View experimental mode
Show the HTLC sequence

Only in this experimental mode, Alice is the HNS seller who locks HNS first and Bob is the HNS buyer who locks BTC second:

  1. Alice generates or receives the secret and shares its hash.
  2. Alice locks HNS with the longer refund window.
  3. Bob independently verifies that lock, then locks BTC with the shorter refund window.
  4. Alice claims BTC, revealing the secret on-chain.
  5. Bob uses that revealed secret to claim HNS.

Use testnet, simnet, regtest, or very small experimental amounts until the wallet-assisted path has been independently proven safe.
